## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need this handled: Juno Customer Signal Synthesizer: a PM dashboard that groups customer feedback into evidence-backed signals, quantifies their commercial impact, and assigns a suggested urgency.. Juno does this inside limits a human set on purpose, and defends every call it makes.

## 01 Context · Data Requirements

**Required sources, and exclusions**

Jira: customer-linked issues and enhancements from the Product and Customer Escalation projects. Juno reads issue ID, title, description, status, linked customer/account ID, labels, comments, created date, and updated date.

Slack: messages from approved customer-signal channels such as #customer-escalations, #enterprise-customer-feedback, and #deal-desk. Juno reads message content, thread replies, author, timestamp, and linked customer or opportunity where identifiable.

Notion — “Customer Feedback Hub”: structured research notes, interview summaries, and account feedback used to validate or enrich signal buckets.

Notion — “Customer Accounts & Revenue”: source of truth for existing customer ARR and canonical account IDs.

Notion — “Active Opportunities”: source of truth for active opportunities, opportunity value, stage, and linked account.

Juno reconciles all sources through the canonical customer or opportunity ID before counting or attaching commercial value.

Excluded

Private Slack DMs and private channels.

Email.

Unstructured meeting recordings or transcripts.

Closed/lost opportunities when calculating current opportunity value.

Revenue or opportunity values mentioned informally in Slack when they conflict with the Notion commercial source of truth.

Feedback with no reliable customer or opportunity identity may inform the wording of a theme but cannot contribute to customer counts, revenue, or opportunity value.

**How fresh must it be**

Jira and Slack: changes reflected in Juno within 5 minutes.

Notion feedback: reflected within 15 minutes.

Customer revenue and active opportunity data: refreshed within 30 minutes.

Customer-count and commercial-value deltas compare the current 30-day period with the previous 30-day period.

If a feedback source is unreachable, Juno keeps the existing dashboard visible but marks it “Partial data — [source] unavailable” and does not claim new or changed signals from that source.

If the commercial source is unavailable or older than 24 hours, Juno must not use the stale value to recalculate rankings. The bucket remains visible, the commercial metric is labelled unavailable, and revenue-based ranking is temporarily disabled rather than estimated.

**The verb list**

calculate_suggested_urgency(bucket) — DRAFT

update_signal_dashboard(bucket, metrics, evidence) — WRITE

flag_signal_for_review(bucket, reason) — WRITE

**Deliberate omissions**

No create_jira_issue() or reprioritize_jira_issue(). A customer signal is evidence for a product decision, not the decision itself. Juno may surface that a problem appears important, but creating or reprioritizing roadmap work remains a PM action.

No send_slack_message(). Juno does not automatically contact Sales, Customer Success, Engineering, or customers based on a derived signal. A false grouping should not create an organizational escalation.

No write access to Notion commercial records. Juno consumes revenue and opportunity data but cannot modify the system of record it uses to justify its own ranking.

## 03 Loop · AI Costs & Latency

**Turn ceiling and escalation**

Ceiling: maximum 3 tool/reasoning rounds per dashboard refresh or signal reclassification.

Juno stops and flags the signal for human review when:

- two sources disagree on the identity of the affected customer or opportunity;

- the commercial value cannot be reconciled to a canonical account or opportunity ID;

- two source-of-truth records provide conflicting current revenue or opportunity values;

- more than 2 required tool calls fail during the same refresh.

**Latency and cost target**

A requested refresh or expanded signal analysis completes in under 10 seconds for at least 95% of requests.

Incremental refresh cost target: ≤ $0.12 per refresh.

Juno should process only new or changed source records during routine refreshes rather than re-reading the full feedback corpus.

## 04 Memory · Data Requirements

**What persists, at what scope**

Team-level memory

The following persists across PMs because the dashboard is a shared product view:

signal bucket name and definition;

Issue vs Enhancement classification;

customer and opportunity IDs associated with each bucket;

evidence references;

historical customer counts;

historical existing revenue and opportunity-value snapshots;

human-approved bucket merges or splits;

human overrides to Juno's classification.

User-level memory

A PM's personal sorting, filters, or expanded rows may persist for that PM only and do not change the shared signal model.

Raw conversational context from one PM's session must never become shared evidence unless it already exists in an approved Jira, Slack, or Notion source.

**Expiry and write rules**

Raw Slack/Jira/Notion content is re-read from source rather than treated as permanent model memory.

Derived commercial metrics expire after 24 hours and must be refreshed from the commercial source.

Signal history is retained for 12 months to calculate changes over time.

Human-approved bucket definitions and merge/split decisions persist until another authorized PM changes them.

A human correction always outranks Juno's previous classification. Juno may later surface new evidence suggesting the correction should be revisited, but it cannot silently overwrite it.

## 05 Permissions · AI Risks & Mitigations

**The permission tier**

| Side-effect class | Tier |
|---|---|
| read | auto |
| draft | auto |
| write | auto |
| send | blocked |

**Justification**

Reads are auto because they are restricted to approved internal sources and create no external side effect.

Drafts are auto because classification and prioritization proposals are reversible and remain internal.

Dashboard writes are auto because continuous synthesis is the product's core value. Requiring a PM to approve every customer-count or revenue delta would recreate the manual work Juno is supposed to remove. The blast radius is limited because Juno writes only to its own derived dashboard, retains evidence, and all changes can be corrected or rolled back.

Sends are blocked because a false signal could unnecessarily escalate an issue, influence another team's priorities, or create an incorrect commercial expectation. The PM decides whether a signal warrants action outside Juno.

## 06 Verification · AI Testing & Measurement

**The check before output ships**

Every dashboard update passes a verification gate before becoming the current signal view.

The verifier checks that:

Every counted customer or opportunity maps to one canonical ID. The same account cannot be counted twice because it appears in Jira, Slack, and Notion.

Every existing revenue value matches “Customer Accounts & Revenue.”

Every opportunity value matches an active record in “Active Opportunities.”

Existing revenue and opportunity value are never combined.

Every displayed delta compares the same metric across the same reporting windows.

Every signal has at least one traceable source reference.

Every “New” signal has source evidence created within the current reporting period.

Suggested urgency is supported by the configured urgency rubric and available evidence rather than revenue alone.

No human-approved bucket merge, split, or classification has been silently overwritten.

**Failure behaviour**

If evidence supports the signal but a commercial metric cannot be verified, Juno keeps the signal visible but removes that metric from ranking and displays:

“Commercial value could not be verified from the current source of truth. This signal is shown without revenue-based ranking.”

If Juno cannot verify that the underlying feedback forms a coherent signal, it does not promote the bucket into the ranked dashboard. Instead, it places it in Needs Review with:

“I found related customer feedback, but I cannot verify that it represents one consistent product signal. Human review is required before this bucket is added or merged.”

The evidence remains visible so the PM can resolve the ambiguity. Juno never invents a customer link, commercial value, or cleaner-looking theme simply to pass the verification gate.
